# Streaming Contract

Treat streams as ordered typed events: content deltas, reasoning deltas, tool-call deltas, usage, metadata, completion, and terminal error. Research UTF-8 fragmentation, SSE comments, multi-line data, partial JSON, cancellation, backpressure, consumer exceptions, and provider errors delivered after HTTP 200.
